Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis project designed to transform raw customer shopping data into meaningful business insights. The analysis focuses on understanding how customers purchase products, how much they spend, how discounts and subscriptions relate to purchasing behavior, and which products and customer groups contribute most to revenue.

The project combines Python, SQL, and Power BI to move from raw data preparation to business analysis and interactive visualization.

Business Objective

The main objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns and identify insights that can help a retail business improve sales, customer engagement, marketing strategies, product performance, and customer loyalty.

The project investigates questions such as:

How does revenue differ between male and female customers?
Do customers using discounts still make higher-value purchases?
Which products receive the highest average ratings?
How does shipping type relate to average purchase amount?
Do subscribed customers spend more than non-subscribers?
Which products have the highest discount usage?
How can customers be segmented based on previous purchases?
What are the most purchased products within each category?
Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
Which age groups contribute the most revenue?

Key Analysis Areas

The project focuses on understanding:

Customer Behavior: Purchasing patterns, spending, and previous purchases.

Product Performance: Sales, ratings, categories, and popular products.

Discount Impact: Discount usage and purchasing behavior.

Customer Loyalty: New, returning, and loyal customer segments.

Subscription Behavior: Differences between subscribers and non-subscribers.

Demographics: Revenue and purchasing behavior across gender and age groups.
